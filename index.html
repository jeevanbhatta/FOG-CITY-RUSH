The real San Francisco waterfront, built from OpenStreetMap. Run the rooftops, zipline off the bridges, race the boulevards, fly a seaplane under the deck and parachute down to the shore.

      Space in the air Seaplane smoke trail

      Drag the left side to run (push all the way to sprint), drag the right side to look. Buttons: jump, slide, grab. Walk up to a car or the seaplane and tap Drive or Fly; in the plane, the buttons become throttle up and down.

      Gamepads work too. Best times, ghosts and postcards are saved on this device.
